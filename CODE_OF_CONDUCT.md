# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Contributors must keep project spaces constructive and professional. Treat people with respect, accept technical disagreement without personal attacks, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publishing private information.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving specific feedback, acknowledging mistakes, and focusing discussion on the work.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, discussions, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents graf.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security advisory form:

https://github.com/pol-cova/graf/security/advisories/new

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be reviewed as privately as the platform permits.
